# Content Verification Gate

Before production release, confirm or remove:

- [ ] Public-facing gym name and logo
- [ ] Exact address and Google Maps pin
- [ ] Phone / WhatsApp number
- [ ] Daily opening hours and ladies-only session hours
- [ ] Privacy and security claims
- [ ] Trainer names, images, qualifications, and consent
- [ ] Programs currently offered
- [ ] Membership prices, admission fees, discounts, and terms
- [ ] Free-trial terms
- [ ] Authentic reviews/testimonials and publication permission
- [ ] Rights/consent for every photo
- [ ] Official social profile links
- [ ] Final domain and hosting target
- [ ] Privacy policy and analytics disclosure

Do not publish sample claims by default.
